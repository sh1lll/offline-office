# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Anyone who is annoyed that a simple task (fix a PDF, convert a file, crop a photo, edit a Word
document) has no simple tool. They do not want to install software, go online, or create an account
for it, and they are uneasy about sending possibly sensitive files to someone else's server. They
arrive with one file and one job, often in a hurry, on a laptop or a phone.

## Product Purpose

An office suite in one HTML file that opens, edits and converts the common file types entirely on
the device. Success: the user drops a file, does the job in seconds, saves it, and never wonders
where their data went. No sign-ups, no uploads, and it keeps working with Wi-Fi off.

## Brand Personality

Aesthetic, modern, minimal, soft. iOS-like: spacious, calm, quietly confident. Clean geometry,
rounded corners, soft blurs, frosted and liquid glass surfaces with a deep, clear hierarchy, so the
document is the brightest, sharpest thing on screen and the tools float around it. Reference:
Apple Pages and Preview (content first, light chrome, native feel).

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS: cream backgrounds, gradient text, hero metrics, identical card grids, eyebrow
  labels on every section.
- Ad-filled online converters: banners, countdowns, "upload to continue", account walls.
- Dense legacy office ribbons that show every command at once.

## Design Principles

1. The document is the hero: chrome recedes (glass, muted ink), content is crisp and opaque.
2. Simple things look simple: one obvious next action per screen; advanced tools tucked into menus.
3. Trust comes from behavior, not badges: no accounts, no uploads, plain explanations where data could be a worry.
4. Soft but exact: generous spacing and round shapes, with precise alignment and consistent sizes.
5. Every screen works on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), including text on glass. Visible focus
rings, full keyboard use, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency alternatives (glass falls back to
solid surfaces), touch targets of at least 40px on phones.
